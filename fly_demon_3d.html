What am I looking at?

The 3D brain (top) is a real connectome — switch between ♀ FAFB v783 (codex.flywire.ai/?dataset=fafb, female full brain, 139,255 neurons proofread) and ♂ MCNS v0.9 (codex.flywire.ai/?dataset=mcns, 166,694 neurons, brain+nerve cord) with the buttons on the left. Neurons are laid out layer by layer (AL → optic ME/LO/LOP → central → motor), every line is a real synapse-weighted edge, green/red = excitatory/inhibitory flashes. The optic lobe receives eight visual-field channels computed from the synapse-level `(x,y,z)` positions and driven by particle positions.

The chamber (bottom) holds a colored ideal gas. The heat jet injects fast particles. The 3D demon is a volumetric horned body with limbs, tail and trident — it hovers on a layered bob/sway/yaw and reacts to every door crossing (eye flash green for a good crossing, red for a bad one, plus a pounce dip, trident kick and startle pulse). The 3D fly is a volumetric fruit-fly proxy with abdomen, thorax, compound eyes, six legs, transparent wings, and a glowing brain marker. These avatars are procedural 3D structures, not anatomical mesh reconstructions. The fly perches on the chamber and its real connectome decides when to open the door. Each door crossing releases dopamine by its physical effect — the brain learns to sort hot left, cold right. Press 🪽 Help against the demon! to see the fly fight back.

I/O contract (sensors → AL currents, motor rate → door) is engineered; the neurons, weights and signs are real data.
